STEP 3 RULES — appraisal_logic only

Add a complete 5-band appraisal_logic block to EVERY objective. Do not change any other field.

Three category scales exist. Match the category AND measure on each objective exactly.

A. MAJOR CRITICAL (only the fixed "Achieve team critical target")
  Rating 5 — Meets Expectations     : Achieves 100% or more (≥ 100% of supervisor's target)
  Rating 4 — Nearly Meets           : Achieves 90–99.9%
  Rating 3 — Partially Meets        : Achieves 80–89.9%
  Rating 2 — Minimally Meets        : Achieves 66–79.9%
  Rating 1 — Unsatisfactory         : Achieves less than 65%

B. CANNOT EXCEED
  Used for: compliance, document quality, accuracy, operational obligations,
            inactive user reduction, UAT completion, SLA adherence.
  Rating 5 — Meets Expectations     : Achieves 100% of target, full compliance
  Rating 4 — Nearly Meets           : Achieves 90–99.9% of target
  Rating 3 — Partially Meets        : Achieves 80–89.9% of target
  Rating 2 — Minimally Meets        : Achieves 66–79.9% of target
  Rating 1 — Unsatisfactory         : Achieves less than 65% of target

  Document quality variant (measure = Quality):
  Rating 5 → Ready after 1st review with minor/no editing
  Rating 4 → Minor editing after 2nd review
  Rating 3 → Minor editing after 3rd review
  Rating 2 → Significant editing after multiple reviews
  Rating 1 → Consistently poor quality

C. CAN EXCEED
  Used for: revenue, active user growth, features, 3rd party integrations,
            time-based document goals, marketing, merchant visits.
  Rating 5 — Exceptional            : Exceeds 150% of plan
  Rating 4 — Above Expectations     : Achieves 125–149.9% of plan
  Rating 3 — Meets Expectations     : Achieves 100–124.9% of plan
  Rating 2 — Below Expectations     : Achieves 65–99.9% of plan
  Rating 1 — Unsatisfactory         : Achieves less than 65% of plan

  Time-based document goals (measure = Time):
  Rating 5 → Action ≤ 1 day
  Rating 4 → Action within 1–2 days
  Rating 3 → Within 3 days
  Rating 2 → Within 4–5 days
  Rating 1 → More than 5 days

  Expense management:
  Rating 5 → Expense < 95% of budget
  Rating 4 → Expense 96–99% of budget
  Rating 3 → Expense = 100% of budget
  Rating 2 → Expense 101–104% of budget
  Rating 1 → Expense > 105% of budget

Every objective MUST include appraisal_logic with rating_5 through rating_1 populated.
