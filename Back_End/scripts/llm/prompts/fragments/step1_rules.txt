STEP 1 RULES — draft text and BSC/LOS mapping only

Do NOT assign weight_percent, measure, target, category, tracking_source,
time_frame, or appraisal_logic in this step. Those are set in Step 2.

1. Cite a BSC KPI only where one genuinely fits this responsibility;
   otherwise set bsc_kpi to "N/A".

2. Add a LOS alignment only where one genuinely fits; otherwise set
   los_alignment to "N/A".

3. Every draft must fit the employee's JD responsibilities and grade
   band — never a responsibility outside the JD or belonging to a
   higher grade band.

4. SMART format: one action verb + one measurable outcome only. Max 12
   words. No second clause (no "and", "by ...ing", "as well as", "in
   addition to" adding a second outcome or mechanism).

5. No numbers, %, $, or timeframe wording anywhere in the objective
   text (no "quarterly", "monthly", "within the next X months", etc.).
   Targets and time_frame are Step 2's job — assume quarterly cadence
   there unless TOR specifies monthly, but never write it into the
   objective sentence itself.

6. Outcome level only — never lead with a tool/system/project name
   (e.g. not "implement a monitoring tool"; instead "Improve Customer
   Satisfaction").


7. No duplicate or overlapping drafts — including different wording
   for the same underlying activity (e.g. "Enhance X" and "Optimize X"
   are the same draft; keep one).

8. Impersonal voice, always. Start with an action verb. Never "I",
    "I will", "I'll", or a job-title lead-in before the action.

9. NO timeframe wording — no "within Q2", "this quarter", "by June",
    "annually", "quarterly", "monthly". time_frame is its own field,
    scoped and set later in Step 2 — do not assume or imply any scope
    here.
